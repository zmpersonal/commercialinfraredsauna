Commercial sauna operations toolkit

Adapt these general records to manufacturer instructions and local requirements.

Opening

  • Inspect cabin, benches, doors and heater guards.
  • Confirm controls and safety devices appear normal.
  • Record defects before opening.

Between sessions

  • Follow the approved cleaning and contact-time procedure.
  • Remove moisture and debris.
  • Log incidents and take unsafe units out of service.

Scheduled review

  • Complete manufacturer-required inspection and service.
  • Review door operation, fasteners and surfaces.
  • Reconcile incident and maintenance logs.
