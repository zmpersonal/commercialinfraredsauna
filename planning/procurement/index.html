Commercial sauna procurement checklist

Verify commercial status in writing before comparing features.

  • Is commercial use expressly permitted for this exact model?
  • Which warranty applies in commercial use?
  • Are heaters, controls, cabin, labor and freight covered separately?
  • What certification/listing applies to this configuration?
  • What are voltage, phase, amperage and circuit requirements?
  • Who handles parts, diagnostics and downtime?
  • Which modifications void the warranty or listing?
