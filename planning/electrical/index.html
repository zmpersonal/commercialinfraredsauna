ELECTRICAL PLANNING

Commercial sauna electrical planning

A pre-design checklist—not circuit-sizing advice.

Collect before design

  • Rated voltage, phase, frequency, watts and full-load current.
  • Required breaker, conductor, disconnect and dedicated-circuit instructions.
  • Controller, lighting, ventilation and accessory loads.
  • Certification details and exact model number.
  • Warm-up schedule and whether multiple units start together.
  • Utility energy and demand tariff, not only average cents/kWh.

Ask the electrician

  • Does the existing panel and service have capacity?
  • How does the adopted code treat the load?
  • Could staggered startup reduce coincident demand?
